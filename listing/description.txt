Pull Tab is the simplest, secure way to talk to your Tab assistant from Even Realities G2.

Triple-tap the temple or the R1 ring, ask your question, and triple-tap to send. The lens says "Sent, reply coming by WhatsApp" (or "by text") and goes dark. Your own Tab answers you by message, in the chat you already use with it, so each answer arrives once, like any other message. Changed your mind? Triple-tap again while "Sent" is showing, and it's cancelled. Double-tap to exit.

Setup takes one message. Open Pull Tab on your phone, copy the setup message, and send it to your Tab in the chat you already use. Your Tab sets up a private relay just for you and tells you when you're connected. There are no accounts to create in Pull Tab and no keys to type.

Private by design:
- Each recording is encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM), and only your own Tab can open it.
- Your recording goes straight from your phone to your own private relay, set up by your own Tab. There is no Pull Tab server in between, and the relay can't listen to your recording.
- Recordings are deleted from your relay as soon as your Tab answers or you cancel, and anything left over expires after 30 minutes (the hosting's encrypted history of it expires within 30 days).
- The mic is on only between your triple-tap and the triple-tap that sends, and never longer than 55 seconds. Nothing is sent without your triple-tap.

You need a Tab assistant (tab.bot).
