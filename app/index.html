Privacy

Your recording is encrypted on this phone. Only your own Tab holds the key: it goes straight from this phone to your own private relay, which your Tab set up for you, and the relay can't open it. Once your Tab opens it, your words are handled like anything else you tell your Tab. It is deleted from your relay once your Tab answers or you cancel, and anything left over expires after 30 minutes. The relay's hosting keeps encrypted database history for up to 30 days, which only your pairing code can open. Pull Tab has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Full notice: pulltab.sites.tab.bot/privacy
