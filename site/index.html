Pull Tab

The simplest, secure way to talk to your Tab assistant from Even Realities G2.

Triple-tap the temple or ring, speak, triple-tap to send, and your own Tab answers you by message, on WhatsApp or by text. The lens just says it's sent, then goes dark.

  1. Install Pull Tab from Even Hub in the Even Realities app.
  2. Open Pull Tab on your phone and copy the setup message.
  3. Send it to your Tab. Your Tab sets up your private relay, and then you're connected.

Your recordings are encrypted on your phone, and only your own Tab can open them. They go straight from your phone to your own private relay: Pull Tab has no server of its own. Privacy notice.
